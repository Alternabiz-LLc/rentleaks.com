Done by the book

The same DNA as every RentLeaks listing

All-in prices, honest fees and fair housing aren't a policy page here — they're how the work is done.

Owner-paid leasing fees

We work for the owner who hires us. Renters never pay a fee for our leasing work, and any fee they do pay is spelled out in the listing — as New York City's FARE Act requires.

Fair housing, every ad

One written screening standard for every applicant. No targeting or preference by any protected characteristic, and paid social runs under the housing ad rules.

Client money kept apart

Rent we collect is held in a client trust account, separate from our own money, and reconciled on every statement.

Homes, not hotel nights

Furnished and co-living units are leased for 30 days or more — the same floor as the rest of RentLeaks.

For out-of-state owners, before listing we check

  • Rental registration or licence with the city or county
  • Lead-based paint disclosure for homes built before 1978
  • Security deposit limits and where the deposit must be held
  • Smoke and carbon-monoxide detector rules
  • Required lease riders and notices (for example window-guard and bedbug notices in New York City)
  • Fee disclosures and who pays the broker (New York City's FARE Act)
  • Rent regulation status and registration
  • Fair-housing wording in every ad

Requirements depend on the city and state; we confirm the ones that apply to your property in writing. This is not legal advice.

Questions

Answered plainly

Who pays the broker fee?

The owner who hires us. We work landlord-side under a written agreement and never charge renters a fee for our leasing work. In New York City the FARE Act makes this the rule, and every fee a renter does pay must be disclosed in the listing.

Are you licensed?

Brokerage and property management are provided by our licensed real estate brokerage. The licence holder, licence number and states appear on every page of this section. Where we are not licensed, we work with a licensed local partner and say so in the proposal.

How do you screen applicants?

With one written standard, applied the same way to every applicant, and within the limits of local law on fees and checks. We never ask about or advertise a preference for a protected characteristic.

Where does the rent go?

Into a client trust (escrow) account kept separate from our own money, then to you with a monthly statement showing rent collected, expenses paid and our fee.

Do you manage short stays?

No hotel nights, ever. Furnished and co-living homes are leased for 30 days or more — the same floor as the rest of RentLeaks.

I live in another state. How do I sign and hand over keys?

Agreements and leases are signed electronically. Keys go to us by courier or a lockbox we install; we send a photo condition report at move-in and move-out.

Which platforms do you advertise on?

The major national rental portals, local listing sites, social marketplaces and RentLeaks — the mix depends on your market and is set out in the proposal. Some portals charge their own fees; we show those separately.

Can you run ads in Europe?

Yes — RentLeaks covers major cities in the UK, Ireland, France, Spain, the Netherlands, Switzerland, Germany and Italy, and listings can run in six languages. Brokerage and management abroad are arranged through licensed local partners.
