# RentLeaks Facebook Page: content to paste

Everything the Facebook Page (facebook.com/rentleakshq) needs, taken from what the website actually says and does. Paste each block into the Facebook field named in its heading.

Rules for every field and post:

- **Describe the home, never the person.** Don't write "ideal for students", "professionals only", "no kids", or anything about religion, origin, age or disability. Fair Housing rules apply to posts and ads as well as listings.
- **Don't advertise the sample catalogue.** The homes on the site today are examples. Don't post them as available, and don't quote a count of "live homes" until real hosts have published.
- **Only promise what you will deliver.** "Reply within one business day" appears below. Keep it only if someone answers Messenger and the Leads inbox every weekday.
- **Booking promises wait for real homes.** Until hosts publish real listings, the viewing and booking tabs are empty, so lead with "Tell us what you need".
- **Links:** every link below carries a source tag, so leads show where they came from in `/admin#leads` and in analytics.

## 6. Four weeks of posts (3 a week)

Post in this order. Each has its link. Add a photo or short video: the cover image, a screen recording of the site, or a real host's photos (with their permission).

**Week 1: what it is**

1. **Mon · Renters.** *Tired of "from $1,200" that turns into $1,650?* Every RentLeaks price is all-in: base rent plus the monthly extras we can see, with one-time fees shown separately. Look for the number you'll actually pay. → `https://rentleaks.com/rent.html?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=wk1_allin`
2. **Wed · Hosts.** *Leaving your lease early?* Post the rest of it on RentLeaks for free. Renters see the remaining months, the Lease Clock, and whether it's an assignment or a sublet. → `https://rentleaks.com/lease-break.html?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=wk1_leasebreak`
3. **Fri · Safety.** *The four steps that stop most rental scams:* 1) see it first, in person or on a live video call; 2) read the lease before any money moves; 3) pay traceably, to a named account (never wire, gift cards or crypto); 4) photograph the condition on move-in day. RentLeaks never takes deposits or rent.

**Week 2: how to use it**

4. **Mon · Search by dates.** Most rental sites ask when you can move in. We also ask when you're leaving. Put in both dates and see only homes that fit your window. → `https://rentleaks.com/rent.html?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=wk2_dates`
5. **Wed · Book a viewing** *(post only once real homes are listed; skip it until then)*. Found a home? Pick up to three times, in person or on video, and the host confirms. Free, from right here. → `https://rentleaks.com/facebook.html?src=fb_post&tab=viewing`
6. **Fri · Stay DNA.** Not sure if you want a room, co-living or a furnished place? Stay DNA asks about city, budget, stay length and the energy you want at home, then ranks every option. It's scored in your browser and isn't a credit check. → `https://rentleaks.com/match.html?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=wk2_staydna`

**Week 3: types of home**

7. **Mon · Rooms.** A private bedroom in a shared home: your own door, split rent. See housemate vibe and private-bath options before you tour. → `https://rentleaks.com/rooms.html?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=wk3_rooms`
8. **Wed · Co-living vs a room.** A room is a bedroom in a regular shared apartment. Co-living is a designed building with per-room inventory, cleaning, and often events or coworking. Both can start at one month. → `https://rentleaks.com/coliving.html?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=wk3_coliving`
9. **Fri · Furnished, 1-month+.** Move in with a suitcase. Furnished homes list what's actually in them, and every stay is 30 days or more: homes, not hotel nights. → `https://rentleaks.com/furnished.html?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=wk3_furnished`

**Week 4: markets and hosts**

10. **Mon · New York.** Since June 11, 2025, a broker hired by the landlord can't charge you the broker fee in New York City (the FARE Act, Local Law 119 of 2024). Every NYC listing on RentLeaks shows who is in the deal, and a fee that shouldn't be there is blocked. → `https://rentleaks.com/rent.html?city=nyc&utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=wk4_nyc`
11. **Wed · Europe and Canada.** From Toronto to Berlin: the same rules everywhere on RentLeaks. All-in rent, a 30-day minimum, and no bait listings. 79 cities and counting. → `https://rentleaks.com/cities.html?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=wk4_cities`
12. **Fri · Hosts.** Renting out a room, managing furnished units or running a co-living building? Listing takes about ten minutes. We check the fees against your city's rules and review every listing, so renters trust what they see. → `https://rentleaks.com/list.html?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=wk4_hosts`

### Three things to settle on the website before sending Facebook traffic

1. **Host prices don't match.** The *For Professionals* page lists $19, $49 and $149 per month. The app's checkout charges $14/week or $60/month per listing, and sponsored placement costs +$10/week or +$35/month. The founder dashboard's revenue estimate uses a third set ($45/$120). Pick one price list before posting prices.
2. **The home page says "69 markets"; the site has 79 cities.** It also shows "0 live homes". This draft says 79 and avoids a homes count.
3. **The type pages call the sample homes "live".** Rooms, Co-living, Furnished, 1-month+, Aparthotel and Lease-break each show "111 live …" or "110 live …", and list sample homes with addresses. Once Facebook sends real renters, those pages should label the homes as examples, or hide them until real hosts publish.
