# TikTok — first four weeks

Two to three videos a week, 20–40 seconds, shot vertical, face-to-camera or
screen-record with a voiceover. Captions are ≤ 150 characters plus hashtags
(the field takes 4,000, but the first line is what people read).

Every script below is: **hook in the first two seconds → one useful thing →
one line about what to do next.** Burn the text on screen; most watching is
muted. Covers are in `marketing/social/tiktok/`.

House rules: describe the home, never who should live in it. Never promise an
apartment or a price. If you name a lease, it has to be a real one, with no
renter's name, face or address.

## 1 · The one that should launch the account

**Cover:** `cover-fare-1080x1920.png`

**Hook (on screen + said out loud):** "If a broker asks you for a fee on their
own listing, that's not a negotiation — it's illegal."

**Script (≈30s)**
> Since June 2025 in New York City, whoever hires the broker pays the broker.
> Landlord's agent lists the apartment? That agent's fee is the landlord's.
> They can't move it onto you, and they have to tell you in writing before the
> viewing if they represent the landlord.
> The one time you do pay is when you hire your own agent — to search, book
> viewings, know the buildings, negotiate. And even then, the fee is whatever
> you agreed, in writing, before the search started.
> So: before you talk to anyone, decide the most you'd pay. That number is your
> cap, and it changes every conversation you have after it.

**On-screen text beats:** "Who hires → who pays" · "Landlord's agent = landlord's fee" · "Your own agent = your fee, agreed FIRST" · "Set your cap"

**Caption:** `The fee rule most NYC renters still don't know. Free 5-page playbook in bio 👆`
`#nycapartments #fareact #renterrights #nycrenting #apartmenthunting`

## 2 · Fastest-to-make video (maths on screen)

**Cover:** `cover-cap-1080x1920.png`

**Hook:** "One month or fifteen percent — same apartment, $2,880 apart."

**Script (≈20s)**
> $3,600 apartment. "One month" is $3,600. "Twelve percent of the year" is
> $5,184. "Fifteen percent" is $6,480. Same apartment, same agent, three
> different answers — and people quote them like they mean the same thing.
> One month is about 8.33% of a year. Convert before you compare, then set a cap
> and make them pitch under it.

**On-screen:** a three-row table that builds as you speak.

**Caption:** `Convert before you compare. Fee-cap worksheet is in the free guide 👆`
`#apartmenthunting #nycrenting #brokerfee #renterstips`

## 3 · "Don't sign that" (highest save rate)

**Hook:** "Four things that mean you should not sign that broker agreement."

**Script (≈35s)**
> One: the fee isn't a number. "Standard" isn't a number.
> Two: it locks you in for six months with no way out. Ninety days, endable by
> email, is normal.
> Three: it asks for money before you've seen anything. Rent and deposits go to
> the landlord, against a signed lease — never to an agent's personal account.
> Four: an apartment is only available to you *if* you sign with them. That one
> is prohibited outright.
> A fair agreement says the fee in dollars, says no lease means no fee, and says
> they can't charge you for homes their own brokerage lists for the landlord.

**Caption:** `Save this before your next viewing. Full checklist in the free playbook 👆`
`#renterrights #nycapartments #apartmenttips #leasesigning`

## 6 · Myth-bust, stitch-friendly

**Hook:** "No-fee doesn't mean nobody's paying a broker."

**Script (≈20s)**
> It means the landlord is. They hired the agent, so it's their cost — that's the
> rule now. What you should be asking is what *you'll* pay in fees at signing:
> application, move-in, anything else. Those have to be disclosed clearly, in the
> listing and in writing before the lease.

**Caption:** `Ask for the itemised fee list before you sign. #fareact #nycrenting #renterrights`

## Posting notes

- **Sound:** use the Business Creative Hub library. A trending track that isn't cleared for commercial use can get a business account's video muted.
- **Comments:** keep the filter word list from PROFILE-KIT.md on. Reply to the fee questions on camera — those replies become videos 9, 10 and 11.
- **Don't** duet or stitch anything that makes a legal claim you can't check.
- **Do** pin videos 1, 3 and 4 to the top of the profile.
- **Link:** TikTok bio link goes to the guide page; for a specific video, say "link in bio" rather than a URL on screen — on-screen URLs aren't clickable and read as spam.
