# Instagram — first four weeks

Two feed posts and two or three stories a week. Captions are written for the
first line to do the work (that's all that shows before "more"). Art is in
`marketing/social/instagram/`; carousels below list one slide per line so you
can build them from the same art plus plain text slides.

Fair housing applies to organic posts exactly as it does to ads: describe the
home, never who should live in it. No stock faces — partner photos are the real
ten on the site.

## Feed 5 · Myth vs fact (carousel, 4 slides)

1. "No-fee means the landlord is doing me a favour." → It means the law puts that fee on them.
2. "The fee is always one month." → It's whatever you agree, in writing, before the search.
3. "If I use a broker I'll see more apartments." → You'll see the ones they can reach — ask which buildings they actually work.
4. "I have to sign an exclusive for six months." → You don't. Ours is 90 days and either side can end it by email.

**Caption**

> Four things renters tell us that aren't quite true — and what's actually the case.

## Reels — three that carry the account

Shoot vertical, 15–25 seconds, caption burned in (most people watch muted).

**Reel 1 · "The two renters"**
- Hook (0–2s): "Same building. One pays $3,600. One pays nothing."
- 3–12s: the rule — who hires, who pays; landlord's agent means the landlord's cost.
- 13–20s: the exception — you hire your own, in writing, first.
- End card: `story-guide-1080x1920.png` — "Free playbook, link in bio."

**Reel 2 · "Convert before you compare"**
- Hook: "One month or 15%? They're not the same."
- Show the maths on screen: $3,600 vs $6,480 on the same apartment.
- End: "Set a cap. Make them pitch under it."

**Reel 3 · "What your agreement must say"**
- Hook: "If your broker agreement doesn't say this, don't sign it."
- Five on-screen lines: the fee in dollars · no lease no fee · no fee on their own landlord listings · 90 days, endable by email · money goes to the landlord, never to the agent's account.
- End: "Whole checklist in the free guide."
